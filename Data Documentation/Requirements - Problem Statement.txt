"Marketplace Performance Intelligence"

Project Statement: The company's leadership wants to know how the business is really performing and what to fix first. Using one year-by-year dataset of real orders, students turn raw files into a clean data model, 9 dashboards, insights, and a storytelling presentation for management.

Brief Project Idea:
"We sell through a marketplace model: customers order, independent sellers ship, and we handle payments and customer experience. Leadership meets next month and I need to answer five things: 
(1) Is revenue growing, and where does it come from? 
(2) Which products and categories matter most? 
(3) Who are our customers and do they come back? 
(4) Are we delivering on time, and does late delivery hurt satisfaction? 
(5) Which sellers and states need attention? 
Please give me dashboards, the key insights, and a short story I can present, with clear recommendations."


Dashboards to get created:

Sales & Revenue: How much revenue per month? Growth month-over-month? Best weekday/hour? Average order value (AOV)?

Products & Categories: Top 10 categories by revenue and by orders? Price vs freight cost? Which categories are growing or shrinking?

Customers: How many unique customers? New vs repeat? Who are the best customers (RFM segments)?

Delivery & Logistics: Average delivery days? On-time vs late %? Which states are slowest? How much late delivery per month?

Customer Satisfaction: Average review score? Share of 1-star reviews? Does late delivery lower the score?

Sellers: Top sellers by revenue? Sellers with the most late orders or worst reviews? How concentrated is revenue?

Geography: Revenue and orders by state and city? Where do customers and sellers sit?

Payments: Which payment types are most used? How many installments? Credit-card installment behavior by order value?

Executive Summary: The 8–10 KPIs a CEO checks in 30 seconds.
