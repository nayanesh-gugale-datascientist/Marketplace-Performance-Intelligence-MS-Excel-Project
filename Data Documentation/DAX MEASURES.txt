RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary)

It is a customer segmentation technique in Data Analytics to understand the behaviour of the customer or the purchasing patterns of customer.

Recency: Measure how recently a customer purchased something.

Frequency: It measures how often a customer purchase something

Monetary: Measures how much money a customer spends.
